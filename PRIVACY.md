# SnapMeasure privacy policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

SnapMeasure is a screenshot and measuring tool made by sARATH. It runs entirely on your PC.

**What it collects:** nothing. SnapMeasure has no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no account.

**Your pictures and measurements** stay on your PC. They are saved only where you choose (or in your save folder),
and copied only to your clipboard when you ask.

**Text recognition (OCR) and PDF pages** use the components built into Windows. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Settings** (capture keys, save folder, file name pattern …) are kept in `SnapMeasure.ini` on your PC.

**Internet:** the portable version goes online only when you click *Check for updates* in the About window, to read the
latest version number from GitHub. The Microsoft Store version never does this; the Store updates it.

**Questions:** open an issue at https://github.com/sARATH-3D/SnapMeasure/issues
